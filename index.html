What you were handed

The name

الحرمين

From ḥaram — sacred, not to be trespassed on. Then the ending -ayn, which does something English can’t: it marks exactly two. Al-Ḥaramayn, the two sanctuaries.

Your name is grammatically incapable of being singular. It needs two of something before it means anything. I don’t think that’s nothing.

On being left alone

On what carried you through the year

Harmain / twenty-one

Everything in the first half was handed to you. The date, the moon that wasn’t full, the name that refuses to be singular. You had no say in any of it.

Everything in the second half you answered yourself, and the picture only exists because you did. That felt like the honest way round.

I won’t pretend I know you well. I know enough to be sure you are loved, by more people than you probably count, and I know I’m glad to have your friendship — I don’t hold it lightly.

Happy twenty-one. I hope this year is gentle with you.

— Moiz
